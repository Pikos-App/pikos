Fast at any size, and calendar sync that behaves.

- **The same speed with fifty pages or half a million.** Opening a page, searching, switching views, and the sidebar's counts don't slow down as your workspace grows. The numbers, and how to check them on your own Mac, are on [the speed page](https://pikos.app/speed).
- **The current-time line moves.** It used to stay where it was when you opened the calendar.
- **Old repeating meetings stay gone.** Series that ended before you connected stay out, each meeting shows once, and a meeting you've ticked through stops showing as live.
- **Meeting links are links.** A Google Meet or Zoom link in a synced event's description is clickable.
- **The command line and MCP speak Markdown**, so an edit from an assistant keeps a page's formatting.

### Calendar

- **Right-click a calendar block** to open its details.
- **iCloud events with only a duration arrive.** They were missing before.
- **A description edited on the Google Calendar website** shows as text, not HTML.
- **Calendar sync works on networks that inspect secure traffic**, like many office networks, as long as your Mac trusts them.
- **An evening meeting from a calendar in another time zone** counts on the day it happens for you.
- **A page at midnight with no end time** shows on the calendar, and a block with no end time reads "1 AM", not "1–1 AM".

### Pages and search

- **Search finds the word after a line break**, in pages written before 0.4.0 too.
- **Images survive a copied workspace**, moved to another Mac or user, and a Markdown export keeps them and your folders.
- **A page trashed from the command line** closes in the app.
- **Quick Add reads more of what you type.** "End of next month" lands on the month's last day, and the connecting words and a final full stop stay out of the title. After Shift+Enter, the cursor lands in the new page's body.

### Command line and MCP

- **More to change.** Move pages between folders, edit tags and subtitles, manage folders and the trash, and skip, move, or reopen one occurrence of a repeating page.
- **Tick off a repeating calendar event** from the command line.
- **Better answers for assistants.** List filters by when a page was created or completed, and search falls back to any of your words when no page holds them all.
