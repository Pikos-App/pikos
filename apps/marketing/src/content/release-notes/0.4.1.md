Fast at any size. Pikos now loads only what's on screen, so opening a page, searching, and switching views take the same time with fifty pages or half a million. The numbers, and how to check them on your own machine, are on [the speed page](https://pikos.app/speed).

### Fixed

- **The current-time line moves.** It used to stay where it was when you opened the calendar.
- **Old repeating meetings stay gone.** Calendar sync no longer brings in series that ended before you connected, and each meeting shows once.
- **Changes from the CLI show up on their own.** Add or edit a page with `pikos` and the open app picks it up, without losing your place in the list.
- **Meeting links are links.** A Google Meet or Zoom link in a synced event's description is clickable.

### CLI and MCP

- **Pages read and write as Markdown.** Editing a page from the CLI or MCP keeps its formatting.
- **More to change.** Move pages between folders, edit tags, manage folders and the trash, and skip, move or reopen one occurrence of a repeating page.

Plus right-click and `Esc` shortcuts on the calendar, and a couple dozen smaller fixes to sync, search, Quick Add, and export.
