Pages link to each other. Type `[[` and part of a title to link a page; the link follows the page through a rename, and a page shows how many others link to it.

### Added

- **Back and forward.** Step through the pages you opened with Cmd+[ and Cmd+], or the mouse's side buttons.
- **Code blocks are coloured** by the language you pick or the one Pikos guesses when you paste.
- **Export one page** as Markdown from its menu or with Cmd+Shift+E.

### Updated

- **Quick Add shows what it read.** The date, priority, tag and folder are coloured where you typed them, and a calendar block's title reads them the same way.
- **A denser calendar**, down to 30px an hour, with short meetings as tall as their time.

### Fixed

- **A save that fails says so**, beside the word count, and retrying sends your edit again.
- **Text is easier to read** in light mode: links, hints and the faint labels all reach the contrast bar.
