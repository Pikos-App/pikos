Ticks on a repeating Google meeting stay ticked.

- **Ticks survive a "this and following" edit in Google Calendar.** Google ends the meeting's series on that day and starts a new one for the rest, so the days you'd already ticked came back as not done. Now they carry over. iCloud and other CalDAV calendars don't say when a series was split, so ticks there still stay behind.
- **The current-time line is right when you leave a page.** Going back to the calendar from the editor used to leave the line where it was when you opened the page.
