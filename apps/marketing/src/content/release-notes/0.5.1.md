Fixes for synced calendars, including one that stopped syncing without saying so.

- **Ticks survive a "this and following" edit.** Changing a repeating event from one date on ends its series there and starts a new one for the rest, so the days you'd already ticked came back as not done. Now they carry over. If the edit also renames the event, they stay behind as before.
- **A calendar that stops syncing tells you.** If a calendar account needs you to sign in again, or Pikos can't reach it for over an hour, a banner over the editor and the calendar says so and offers the fix. Reminders from that calendar still come, with a note that the event may have changed. Before, the only sign was a line in Settings.
- **Reconnecting a calendar doesn't bring back old events.** After a long gap, Google can resend events from long ago, and each one became an open page at its own date, some over a year back. Events that were over before the last week now stay out.
- **The current-time line is right after the editor, and after sleep.** Going back to the calendar from a page left the line where it was when you opened the page, and waking the Mac could leave it behind too. Now it catches up within a few seconds.
- **The log says more about calendar sync.** If you report a sync problem, pikos.log now shows what sync left out, removed or carried over, and it keeps older history instead of starting over each time it fills.
