Fixes for synced calendars.

- **Ticks stay ticked** when you change a repeating event "this and following".
- **A calendar that stops syncing says so**, above the editor and the calendar, with a button to reconnect. Its reminders still come, marked as maybe out of date.
- **Reconnecting a calendar doesn't bring back old events.**
- **The current-time line is right** when you leave a page and when your Mac wakes.
- **The log says more about calendar sync**, which helps when you report a sync problem.
