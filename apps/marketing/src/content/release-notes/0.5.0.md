Pages link to each other, nothing you type gets lost, and long pages are fast.

- **Page links.** Type `[[` and part of a title to link a page. The link follows the page through a rename, and a page shows how many others link to it.
- **No lost edits.** Quitting right after typing keeps your last words, and a page changed somewhere else while you type keeps both edits.
- **Long pages are fast.** A page of hundreds of thousands of words opens at once, and typing and Find keep up.
- **Back and forward** through the pages you opened, with Cmd+[ and Cmd+] or the mouse's side buttons.

### Pages and links

- **Link a page** with `[[`. Only pages that exist are offered, and the link shows the page's current title wherever you read it, including in a Markdown export.
- **See what links here.** A page's details row counts the pages linking to it and lists them.
- **Export one page** as Markdown from its menu, the command palette or Cmd+Shift+E. Finder opens on the file.
- **Code blocks have a language.** Pick one and the code is coloured for it. Paste code in and Pikos guesses.

### Writing and saving

- **Quitting keeps your last words.** Cmd+Q saves what you just typed before the app closes.
- **Two edits to one page both survive.** If the command line, an assistant or another window changes the page you're typing in, Pikos merges the two instead of keeping only yours.
- **An open page shows outside changes.** Change a page from the command line while it's open and untouched, and the new text appears in place.
- **A failed save says so.** "Not saved" appears beside the word count, and clicking it tries again.
- **A page from a newer Pikos opens read-only**, with a note to update, so an older copy of the app can't overwrite it.

### Long pages

- **Open at once.** A very long page shows its first screen right away and draws the rest a moment later.
- **Typing stays quick.** A keystroke on a page of half a million words reaches the screen in about a tenth of a second.
- **Find answers fast**, in about a third of a second on the longest pages, and it finds a word even when part of it is bold or italic.
- **The word count catches up when you pause**, instead of slowing every keystroke.

### Keyboard and lists

- **Escape leaves the editor.** The page stays open, Space completes it and the arrows move between pages. A second Escape goes to the calendar.
- **Shift-click selects what you see** between the two clicks, Completed included.
- **After deleting the open page**, the arrows carry on from where it was.
- **One undo for a multi-page delete**, and emptying the trash asks once instead of making you type "delete".
- **The command palette ranks better.** A command whose word starts with what you typed comes first, with the letters marked.

### Quick Add and dates

- **Quick Add shows what it read.** The date, priority, tag and folder are coloured where you typed them.
- **A calendar block's title reads the same way**, so "Standup #work every weekday" tags and repeats the block.
- **"Morning pages tomorrow 9am"** keeps "Morning pages" as the title, in the app and from the command line.
- **"Next week"** means the coming Monday.
- **An all-day page due today reads "Today"** in amber.

### Calendar

- **Denser or roomier.** Calendar density goes up and down in steps, down to 30px an hour, from Option-Cmd-= and Option-Cmd-- or the View menu.
- **Multi-day events in month view** draw as one bar per week.
- **Events draw with the grid** when you open the calendar, instead of popping in after it.
- **The current-time line is where it should be** when you come back to Pikos from another app.
- **A block's time that doesn't fit** ends in "…" instead of being cut mid-letter.
- **The all-day strip grows** with the calendar's text size.
- **A calendar event's agenda arrives as a bullet list**, and its links stay links when you add a changed description.
- **A focus session running when you quit** is recorded with its real end time.

### Look and safety

- **Easier to read.** Text, links, hints and buttons meet the contrast bar in both themes, and a priority is the same colour everywhere.
- **A damaged workspace says so** and offers to restore a backup, instead of failing quietly.
- **A workspace a newer Pikos updated** tells you to update the app.
- **A reminder macOS didn't show** appears in Recent notifications as not shown, with a button to the setting.
- **A Markdown import names any image it couldn't find.**

### Command line and MCP

- **Edit one section of a page** by its heading: add to it, insert under it or replace it, leaving the rest untouched.

0.5.0 upgrades your workspace and 0.4.1 can't open it afterwards. Pikos backs up the old one first.
