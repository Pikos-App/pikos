The first public release of Pikos: notes, tasks, and calendar in one app, stored on your device.

- **Every page is a note and a task.** Write in it, give it a date, tick it off.
- **Quick Add reads plain English**, so "call the dentist tomorrow 3pm !high #health" files itself.
- **A week calendar** you drag pages onto, with repeating pages and reminders.
- **Your data stays on your device.** No account, no sign-up, no tracking. The only thing Pikos sends is an update check, and you can turn it off.

<!-- more -->

### Editor

- **Rich text that speaks Markdown.** Headings, lists, checklists, code blocks, quotes, tables, and images, and pasted Markdown turns into formatting.
- **Type `/` to insert any block**, or select text for a formatting toolbar.
- **Links** turn live when you paste them, and a click edits them.
- **Find in page** highlights every match.
- **Saves as you type.**

### Pages and organization

- **Folders and tags.** Folders take a colour, and a tag is created as you type it.
- **Four priorities**, from Urgent to Low, and every view keeps its own Completed section.
- **Today and Inbox.** Today holds what's scheduled or overdue, Inbox what has no folder, each with a count in the sidebar.
- **Sort and drag.** Sort by hand, date, title, or priority, remembered per view, and drag pages to reorder or file them.
- **A subtitle on any page**, shown in the list and on the calendar.
- **Deleting can be undone.**

### Calendar

- **Drag to create, move, or resize** a block. Moving keeps its length.
- **Repeating pages**, daily to yearly or your own pattern, with any one date moved on its own.
- **Catching up on a repeating page** asks whether to skip the missed days or go through them one at a time.
- **Reminders** arrive as desktop notifications, as early as you choose.
- **`T` jumps to today**, and the arrows move between weeks.

### Search

- **Search titles and text at once**, with title matches first and your words highlighted.

### Getting around

- **Three resizable panels**: sidebar, list, and editor or calendar, with the calendar a toggle away from the open page.
- **A shortcut for every main action**, all listed in Settings.
- **`pikos://` links** open a page, Today, Inbox, or a pre-filled Quick Add.
- **First launch opens a tutorial** you work through, not an empty screen.

### Import and export

- **Bring in an Obsidian vault.** Markdown files become pages and your folders carry over. CSV imports too.
- **Export any time** as a SQLite backup, a Markdown folder, or CSV, all readable without Pikos.

### Look

- **Light, dark, or follow the system** on macOS.
- **The window opens where you left it**, and Pikos updates itself, with Check for Updates in the app menu.
