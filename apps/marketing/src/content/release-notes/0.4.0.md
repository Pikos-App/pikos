Your real calendar, inside Pikos. Connect the calendar you already use and the meeting you're walking into becomes a page you can write notes on.

- **Calendar sync** with Google Calendar, iCloud, or any CalDAV server. Your events arrive as pages you can write on, tag, and search, and nothing you do in Pikos is sent back out. [How to set it up](https://pikos.app/calendar-sync).
- **Focus timer.** Start a session from any page and Pikos narrows to just that page until you're done. It charts your focus time by week.
- **Text size** for the interface, the editor, and the calendar, each on its own. `Cmd +` and `Cmd -` (`Ctrl` on Linux) size whichever panel you're looking at.
- **An MCP server**, so an AI assistant can read and write your pages with no Pikos account or server in the middle. [What it does](https://pikos.app/cli).

### Calendar

- **Repeating pages and repeating meetings work the same way.** Moving, skipping, or deleting one asks the same question: just this one, or the whole series.

0.4.0 upgrades your workspace and 0.3.1 can't open it afterwards. Pikos backs up the old one first, so nothing is lost either way.
